How it works
  • Keep this page open, with the phone plugged in. The screen stays on while an alarm is set.
  • When the alarm goes off, put the phone on the floor about 1.5–2 m away, side-on to you, so the camera can see your whole body.
  • A rep counts when your elbows bend past ~90° with your body horizontal, then straighten again.
  • Everything runs on your device. No video is uploaded.
